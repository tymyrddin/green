Cases: the record made specific
================================

The rest of this model describes surveillance in general terms: the legal openings, the
adversaries, the patterns of use. The pages here are the particulars. Each is a documented,
recent, European case, cited to its primary sources, and each shows the model operating
rather than merely asserting that it does.

None of them required a leak from inside a spy agency to surface. They came from investigative
journalists, a forensic lab, security researchers, and the officials and reporters caught up in
them. That is the part worth holding onto: the evidence for how surveillance works in the EU is,
increasingly, in the open.
