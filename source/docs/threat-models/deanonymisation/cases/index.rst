Cases: re-identification demonstrated
======================================

The rest of this model describes re-identification in general: the data that makes it
possible, the adversaries who do it, the techniques they use. The pages here are the
particular. Each is a documented demonstration, from the research literature or the public
record, that "anonymous" is a claim about effort, not a property of data.

None of them is hypothetical. They are the worked proof behind the model's central point:
strip the names, and the data still names people, given a little auxiliary information, a
public database, or a capable model.
