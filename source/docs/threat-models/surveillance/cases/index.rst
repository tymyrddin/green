Cases: the record made specific
================================

Surveillance in the EU can be set out in general terms: the legal openings, the
adversaries, the patterns of use. The particulars are documented,
recent, European cases, cited to their primary sources.

None of them required a leak from inside a spy agency to surface. They came from investigative
journalists, a forensic lab, security researchers, and the officials and reporters caught up in
them. The evidence for how surveillance works in the EU is, increasingly, in the open.
